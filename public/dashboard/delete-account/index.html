Madadgaar Account Deletion

App/Developer: Madadgaar

This page explains how users of the Madadgaar app can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open Madadgaar app or website and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Dashboard -> Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE and submit the request.

Direct link: https://madadgaar.com.pk/dashboard/delete-account

What data is deleted

  • Profile details (name, email, phone, username, address, CNIC where applicable).
  • Authentication/session access for the deleted account.
  • Access to user dashboard data tied to that account.

What data may be retained

  • Minimal records required for legal, fraud prevention, financial, security, and regulatory compliance.
  • Operational logs and audit traces that may contain limited account identifiers.

Retention period

Retained compliance/audit information may be stored for up to 90 days, unless a longer period is required by law.

Support contact

If you cannot access your account, contact support to request deletion: support@madadgaar.com.pk
